## Generation prompt

Create one clean, minimal logo symbol for Meshmatch, a project comparing how AI coding harnesses reconstruct 3D objects from reference images. Logo-brand asset, production icon, square composition. A distinctive compact geometric monogram M suggested by two matching folded mesh surfaces; just a few bold precise strokes or flat facets, a subtle spatial/3D suggestion, strong negative space, legible at 24px. Warm white monochrome mark on a pure black background, centered and occupying about 78% of the square. Flat graphic design. No text, no lettering outside the symbol, no slogan, no mockup, no texture, no gradient, no glow, no shadow, no mascot, no brain, no sparkles, no generic AI imagery. Understated and confident.
